# ShopSphere

An enterprise-grade E-Commerce Backend built with Spring Boot, designed using a modular monolithic architecture and industry-standard backend development practices.

## Client Background & Problem Statement

**Client:** ShopSphere Pvt. Ltd.  
**Challenge:** ShopSphere is a growing online retail company historically reliant on physical stores. Due to rising digital demand, they faced major operational hurdles:
1. **Channel Expansion:** Lacked an online platform to capture digital traffic 24/7.
2. **Scalability Bottlenecks:** Needed an architecture capable of scaling smoothly from an initial **5,000 daily users** to hundreds of thousands without a complete system rewrite.
3. **Inventory Inconsistency:** Risk of race conditions and overselling during peak traffic surges.
4. **Future-Proofing:** Needed a clean enterprise-standard backend designed to cleanly integrate future mobile apps, third-party logistics, and microservices.

---

## Business Goals & Core Solutions

The ShopSphere backend solves these challenges by providing:
* **Secure User Management:** Robust customer registration and authentication.
* **Catalog & Inventory Control:** Administrative tools to manage products while guaranteeing strict transactional safety to prevent overselling.
* **High-Speed Product Discovery:** Optimized search mechanisms for customers.
* **Safe Order Processing:** End-to-end transactional order placement and tracking.
* **Management Reporting:** Built-in reporting capabilities to give leadership real-time sales and performance analytics.
